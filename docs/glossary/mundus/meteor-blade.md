## 定义

传说中斩落星辰的兵器，由[铁炉工匠会](/glossary/mundus/ironforge-guild)世代保存于[铁炉堡](/locations/mundus/ironforge-hold)。[铁炉堡事变](/events/mundus/ironforge-incident)后被[灰烬之手](/factions/mundus/ashen-hand)夺走。
